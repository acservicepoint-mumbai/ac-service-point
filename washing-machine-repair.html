Washing Machine Repair

Whether you’re looking for washing machine maintenance, washer repair AC Service Point Services can help. We’re your best option for quick and easy local repair and maintenance of front loading, top-load and portable washing machines.

When you start facing problems with your washing machine, it is advisable to book a repair service right away to avoid the risks of further complications or damage. No matter the issue, AC Service Point can provide you with cost-efficient and high-quality washing machine repair service. Our skilled technicians are capable of diagnosing your washing machine and dryer and carrying out repairs as quickly and efficiently as possible.

Contact us immediately if you face any of the following issues:
  • Washing Machine is not turning on.
  • Washer is not spinning normally or at all.
  • The agitator does not work properly.
  • There is leakage of water.
  • Washer isn’t draining water.
  • Washing Machine is making strange or unwanted noises.
  • Washing Machine is vibrating excessively or abnormally.
  • Clothes Dryer is not working well.
  • Wash cycles leave strange spots on clothes.
  • Machine displays error / Main Power Control Board (PCB) needs repair.

We have a team of highly-qualified professionals who can fix even the most complex issues in your washing machine. Whether you have a front load or a top load washer, a semi-automatic or a fully automatic washing machine, we have certified experts in service for you. Our technicians are well-trained and have the necessary experience and expertise to repair all major brands and models of washing machines.

In addition to our renowned washing machine repair service, At our AC Service Point Center we have Specialist in all types laundry equipments, including front load washers, top load washers, washer dryers, stackable washers, washer dryer combos, all-in-one combination washers & dryers, heavy duty dryers, commercial washer dryers, tumble dryer, and others.
