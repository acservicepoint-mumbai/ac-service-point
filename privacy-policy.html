Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

At AC SERVICE POINT (Proprietorship managed by MOHD ASIF KHAN), accessible from our website, accessible services, and booking forms, protecting the privacy of our visitors and clients is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and how we keep it secure.

1. Information We Collect

When you fill out our online inquiry or booking form to request repair, installation, or maintenance services, we collect only the necessary personal information required to deliver our doorstep services. Specifically, we collect:

  • Full Name: To address you and confirm your booking reservation.
  • Mobile Number: To contact you via call or WhatsApp for appointment scheduling and technician dispatch.
  • Service Required: To assign the appropriate technician specialized in Split AC, Window AC, Cassette AC, VRF/Ductable AC, Refrigerator, Washing Machine, or Microwave Oven servicing.
  • Area / Society / Location Address: To verify service availability across Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Vasai, Virar, Nalasopara, and surrounding areas.

2. How We Use Your Information

We strictly use the collected details for operational business purposes, including:

  • Providing, operating, and maintaining our repair and maintenance services.
  • Scheduling doorstep technician visits to your specified address.
  • Communicating service updates, pricing, and inquiry follow-ups.
  • Preventing fraudulent inquiries and maintaining service security.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing & Protection

We do not sell, trade, rent, or lease your personal information to third-party marketing companies or advertisers. Your contact details are strictly kept internal and are shared only with our assigned technicians for the purpose of completing your service request.

4. Compliance with Advertising Policies

In alignment with search engine advertising guidelines (including Google Ads policies), we do not engage in dishonest user data collection. We do not harvest sensitive financial information, banking credentials, or private identity numbers through our website form.
