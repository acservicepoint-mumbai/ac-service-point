AC Repair

Air conditioners are apparently the most essential home appliance at home and have become a necessity rather than a luxury in modern times. But like any appliance, an AC can break down at the most inconvenient times. Don’t let your AC let you down when you need it most. If you’re looking for professional and reliable Air Conditioner repair services, your search ends with AC Service Point.

We offer high-quality and efficient AC repairs helping you get back your comfort again in no time. We also specialize in comprehensive AC services including repair, maintenance, and cleaning at competitive prices in residential as well as commercial settings. AC Service Point has highly qualified air conditioner service and repair team who possess the knowledge and the qualification to impeccably repair today’s highly advanced air conditioners like Inverter AC.

Some common problems with Air Conditioner we regularly resolve include:
  • AC does not power on.
  • AC is leaking water / Condensation Problems.
  • Clogged or Dirty Filter.
  • Drainage line is clogged.
  • Compressor not working.
  • Worn-out, corroded, or frozen coils.
  • Ice formation in the unit.
  • Gas Leakage / Refrigerant Leakage.
  • Strange noises.
  • Capacitor problems.
  • AC not cooling enough or at all.

If you’re being troubled by any of these or some other problem in your AC, our technicians will efficiently diagnose the issue and provide a lasting solution as quickly as possible. At AC Service Point, we have experience repairing all makes and models of air conditioning units, including Split AC, Window AC, Tower AC, VRF AC, Portable AC, Cassette AC, etc. for most major brands in the market.

On time service delivery, transparent prices, service warranty and highly experienced & customer satisfaction driven team makes AC Service Point the best in class provider of AC Service, AC Repair and AC Installation. Our customer-centric approach, competence and high levels of professional integrity make us your ideal choice for all AC service, repair & installation related works.
